About Sarnadip Mandal

Sarnadip Mandal is a professional guitarist, performer, and dedicated music educator with over 10 years of stage performance experience and more than 6 years of teaching expertise.

As a graduate of Rabindra Bharati University, he combines academic musical knowledge with practical performance skills to help students become confident and expressive musicians.

Whether you are a complete beginner or an advanced learner, every lesson is designed to develop technique, creativity, musical understanding, and performance confidence.
